## The Peanut Butter Sandwich

## Assumptions
- bread, PB, knife are all present

## Steps
- undo twist tie on bread bag
- open bread bag
- grab knife
- cut bread into slices with knife
- put down knife
- open peanut butter jar
- remove foil seal
- grab knife
- scoop peanut butter out with knife
- spread peanut butter onto one slice of bread
- scoop more peanut butter with knife
- spread peanut butter onto second slice of bread
- put knife down
- stack slice 1 of bread onto slice 2 of bread

## Done When
- Bread, peanut butter, Bread, all on the plate or table, in one piece with 3 layers
